# Mission

## Purpose

**Tech Networking and Games (TNaG)** exists to bring people in the tech
community together through games — most prominently the **Corporate Magic: The
Gathering League** — turning casual play into genuine professional networking
and friendships.

The website is the community's public home. It exists to:

- **Promote the Corporate MTG League** — showcase active and past leagues, drive
  sign-ups, and give players a place to follow along.
- **Share results and stats** — surface league standings, trades, and analytics
  (via embedded Domo dashboards) so players can track how a season is going.
- **Tell the community's story** — publish blog posts recapping leagues, events,
  and the broader "meet people, play games, build your network" philosophy.
- **Announce events** — a calendar and event pages keep members informed about
  what's coming up.

## Who it's for

- **Prospective players** deciding whether to join a league.
- **Current players** checking standings, trades, and league details.
- **The wider tech community** discovering the group through blog content and
  networking messaging.

## Guiding principles

- **Networking first.** Games are the vehicle; the real product is relationships
  and career connections within the tech community.
- **Low-maintenance publishing.** Adding a league or a blog post should be a
  matter of dropping in a CSV row or a Markdown file — no code changes required.
- **Static and durable.** The site is a static export with no runtime server,
  so it is cheap, fast, and reliable to host.

## What success looks like

- New leagues and blog posts can be published quickly by non-developers editing
  data files.
- Players can easily find the league they care about and its live stats.
- The site loads fast, works without JavaScript for core content, and requires
  minimal operational upkeep.
