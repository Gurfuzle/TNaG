## **What is a**** _Magic: The Gathering_****Corporate League?**

  

A league in _Magic: The Gathering_ is a month-long tournament using sealed packs from a given set. Each player starts with the same number of packs and builds a 40 card deck. In our case, we start with a pre-release kit. Over the course of four weeks, people may add more packs. One per week, after the first, and one for each 3 losses up to a cap of 12 packs total. Games are played best-of-one and players are encouraged to not play the same person twice in a row. 

  

  

![People playing Magic the Gathering](/assets/images/blog/kamigawa-neon-dynasty-corporate-league-1.jpg)

  

  

This format works well for networking! Everyone is on an even playing field. Each person gets their cards from the same number of sealed packs. Whether you are new to the game or have played for years, it doesn't matter. League games are best of one and can play quickly, to fit in your schedule. League members can play people at their own company, meet and play with other companies or play against each other online over Discord. Not playing the same person twice in a row helps to vary things up and meet new players across the league.

  

As the league progresses, when someone wins a game they will record that game through a link provided after the league begins. Stats will show on the league page (see our past league pages [_here_](https://www.technetworkingandgames.com/league-standings)).

  

At the end of the league, we will hold a tournament for players from all companies to come and compete for the trophy and the glory that comes with being the corporate champions of the set. Winners will have their company name, the set, and date put on a plaque on the trophy and the opportunity to display the trophy until the next set. In addition, we will have a small number of promo cards to give out to people who participated throughout the league. These will go to people who played the most unique opponents, changed their deck colors the most often, etc. However, to win a promo card, you must be present at the final tournament!

## **When Do Things Happen?**   

For this league, games will begin Monday, February 21st and run through Friday, March 18th. Weekly packs go out on February 28th, March 7th, and 14th. The final will be held on March 19th at 1:00 p.m. at [_Game Grid in Lehi_](http://www.gglehi.com). ## **How Do I Play on Discord?**

  

With the world as it is, many of us are now used to video conferencing. Playing a game of _Magic: The Gathering_ over something like [_Discord_](https://discord.com/) or [_SpellTable_](https://spelltable.wizards.com/) is just as easy. You will need a computer with a camera setup where your play surface is visible. You can use our discord channel to find others who are looking for a game and go from there.

## **Join as an Independent**

##   

Are you not with a company or not 3 or more from your company? Not a problem! We will have a number of people playing as independents (Someone not a part of a team from a company). You can participate for the league during the month over discord or in person. If you are interested in participating in the final tournament, join with other independents to create a new team or join an existing company's team.
