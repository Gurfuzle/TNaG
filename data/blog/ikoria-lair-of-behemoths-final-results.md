# Ikoria: Lair of Behemoths Final Results!

  * Michael Swensen
  * Jun 25, 2020
  * 1 min read

  

![](/assets/images/blog/ikoria-lair-of-behemoths-final-results-0.jpg)

The Ikoria: Lair of Behemoths league has come and gone and the final tournament was held last week. Thank you to all of the players that participated for making this a success!

  

Now, we are happy to announce that the winners of the first place trophy for this round goes to [_Axomo_](https://axomo.com/)! The scores were close this time around, between Axomo and [_Domo_](http://www.domo.com) , with Axomo pulling ahead. So they will have the opportunity to have a plaque with their name put on the trophy and to proudly display it until the next league final!

  

In addition to our corporate trophy, we did have a number of other winners! Throughout the league we have several challenges that can win players promo cards. To help recognize more people, we only allow each person to "win" one award. To see the final standings, you can check out our league page [_here_](https://www.technetworkingandgames.com/ikoria-lair-of-behemoths-league). With that in mind, this leagues winners are:
