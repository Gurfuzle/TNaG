Loyalty makes you family in the classic Gangster genre. With the new set, _Streets of New Capenna,_ we enter a world ruled by five such families. Come join our family! ## **What is a**** _Magic: The Gathering_****Corporate League?**

  

A league in _Magic: The Gathering_ is a month-long tournament using sealed packs from a given set. Each player starts with the same number of packs and builds a 40 card deck. In our case, we start with a pre-release kit. Over the course of four weeks, people may add more packs. Excluding the first week, one pack can be added each Monday and one pack for each 3 losses up to a maximum of 12 packs total. Games are played best-of-one and players are encouraged to not play the same person twice in a row. 

![Networking through Play](/assets/images/blog/streets-of-new-capenna-corporate-league-1.jpg)

  

This format works well for networking! Whether you are new to the game or have played for years, it doesn't matter. Each person gets a random set of cards from the same number of sealed packs. League games are best-of-one and can play quickly allowing them to fit in your schedule. League members can play people at their own company, meet and play with other companies, or play against each other online over Discord. Playing as many people as you can helps to vary things up and meet new players across the league.

  

As the league progresses, when someone wins a game they will record that game through a link provided after the league begins. Stats will show on the league page (see our past league pages [_here_](https://www.technetworkingandgames.com/league-standings)).

  

  

![Cabretti](/assets/images/blog/streets-of-new-capenna-corporate-league-2.jpg)

  

  

At the end of the league, we will hold a tournament for players from all companies to come and compete for the trophy and the glory that comes with being the corporate champions of the set. Winners will have their company name, the set, and date put on a plaque on the trophy and the opportunity to display the trophy until the next set. In addition, we will have a small number of promo cards to give out to people who participated throughout the league. These will go to people who played the most unique opponents, changed their deck colors the most often, etc. However, to win a promo card, you must be present at the final tournament! 

  

![New Capenna](/assets/images/blog/streets-of-new-capenna-corporate-league-3.jpg)

  

  

## **When Do Things Happen?**   

For this league, games will begin Monday, May 2nd and run through Friday, May 27th. Weekly packs go out on May 9th, 16th, and 23rd. The final will be held on May 28th, time and location yet to be determined. Packs are opened on the honor system, but speaking from experience you will enjoy the league more as you stick to the schedule.

## **How Do I Play on Discord?**

  

With the world as it is, many of us are now used to video conferencing. Playing a game of _Magic: The Gathering_ over something like [_Discord_](https://discord.com/) or [_SpellTable_](https://spelltable.wizards.com/) is just as easy. You will need a computer with a camera setup where your play surface is visible. You can use our discord channel to find others who are looking for a game and go from there

  

  

![Sphinx of New Capenna](/assets/images/blog/streets-of-new-capenna-corporate-league-5.jpg)

  

  

  

## **Other Ways to Play?**

Are you not able to play in person and don't have the setup to play over webcam? There are still ways you can play! You are welcome to enter your deck into [_Untap.in_](https://untap.in/) and play that way. Alternatively, if you have the cards in [_Magic: The Gathering Arena_](https://magic.wizards.com/en/mtgarena?v=4PAZ1mgzwmLDNSae2zfZ5F&gclid=CjwKCAjwo8-SBhAlEiwAopc9W8uKVlMm9p3nZY7M9QmG4OXasv7AXld7GpEaKjHhI5azTD4RkVShghoC_poQAvD_BwE) and so does your opponent, you are welcome to play that way as well.

## **Join as an Independent**

##   

Are you not with a company or not 3 or more from your company? Not a problem! We will have a number of people playing as independents (Someone not a part of a team from a company). You can participate for the league during the month over discord or in person. If you are interested in participating in the final tournament, join with other independents to create a new team or join an existing company's team.
