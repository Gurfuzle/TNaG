# A Modern Resume in a Post Shutdown World

  * Michael Swensen
  * Aug 21, 2020
  * 1 min read

Updated: Mar 5, 2024

When the world first shut down back in March, I don't think anyone fully knew what to expect. We had several people in our networking group who found themselves looking for new opportunities. Fortunately, there were many fantastic recruiters who we were able to connect with in their search. I was able to witness several people go through this process, and with that I learned just how important a good resume can be. 

  

A couple of months ago I shared a link for an article on the [_"dos and don'ts"_](https://www.technetworkingandgames.com/post/is-it-time-to-dust-off-your-resume) of resume writing. After reading that article, Veronica Edgar, a recruiter and a friend of mine, shared these pointers from Tek Systems with me on modernizing your resume. Keeping your resume up to date and ready to go is so important with everything going on. Things seem like they can change overnight, and it is important that you are ready for whatever may come. Take a look and see what you can do to keep your resume up to date!
