# Is It Time to Dust Off Your Resume?

  * Michael Swensen
  * May 4, 2020
  * 1 min read

Updated: Mar 6, 2024

  

  

![Four people greeting and talking](/assets/images/blog/is-it-time-to-dust-off-your-resume-0.jpg)

  

With everything going on, there are a lot of people looking for new opportunities and changes. The opportunities are out there, but if you find yourself looking, it is important that you do everything you can, to make yourself attractive to employers as well. 

  

For many of us, we haven't touched our resume since we accepted our last position. If you need to dust off that resume, here are some tips on what to do to get it ready to send out to your next potential employer!
