# Corporate Magic League - Throne of Eldraine!

  * Michael Swensen
  * Oct 2, 2019
  * 3 min read

Updated: Oct 5, 2019

Does your company, school, or organization have people who play Magic the Gathering? Looking for a way to bridge departments in your company? Wanting to connect with other students in your field? Would your group be interested in participating in a friendly competition with other organizations along the Wasatch Front that share a love for tech? Join us this October for the Corporate Magic League - _Throne of Eldraine_! The league will run from **Monday, October 6th until Friday November 1st.**

###   

### **What is a Magic League?**

A [_Magic League_](https://mtg.gamepedia.com/League) is a tournament style many local game stores run with each new set. We alter it slightly to best fit a work environment and the needs of our group. During the league, each participant builds a deck and plays on their schedule over the course of four weeks to refine that deck. They start with three packs from the set being played, then as they play, for each three games they lose, they can add another pack to their card pool. At the beginning of each week after the first, each player may also add a pack to their pool. Optionally, at the beginning of the league the company may choose to draft the cards, or hold a draft in addition to the original three packs with three more packs. For the purpose of the multi company league, we cap each player at twelve packs. Cards obtained outside of the twelve sealed packs cannot be added to the pool, and players cannot trade cards during the event.

  

Decks have a forty card minimum, and games played during the league should be best of one. Please try to avoid having the same players play against each other twice in a row. This is both helps limit the time a game takes to make lunch games quick and easy to fit in, and encourages players to network with others within your company throughout the event.

  

At the end of the four weeks, we will schedule a cross company tournament, where each participating company will be invited to come and play in a three round swiss style tournament using their league decks. The company with the highest total score among their top five ranking players wins and can take the corporate league trophy and have their company name, set name, and date put on the trophy!

### **How Does My Company Join?**

We ask that your company have a minimum of five participants. Also, you will need to supply your own cards, we recommend going through [_Game Grid in Lehi_](http://gglehi.com) , but however you choose to purchase the packs is fine. They do need to be sealed packs of _Throne of Eldraine_ , no repacks or loose product.

  

Would your company be willing to host the final tournament? Show off your space and have the home field advantage? Let us know when you sign up and we can work out the final location.

  

To sign up for your company to join the _Throne of Eldraine_ League, please fill out our [_sign up form_](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfuN4TYeiZoBhCNYC1SlTuFnyaaBY3LvwE1voBr6gxunzwdHg/viewform?usp=sf_link).

  

### **Final Note**

The _Throne of Eldraine_ league will kick off next Monday (October 6th) and will end Friday, November 1st. You are welcome to join now, or anytime during those four weeks to be eligible to come to the corporate tournament. 

  

Overall, Magic Leagues are a great, casual way to build connections within your own company or organization, and your network outside of it. We hope to see you join us! For any questions, please email technetworkingandgames@gmail.com.
