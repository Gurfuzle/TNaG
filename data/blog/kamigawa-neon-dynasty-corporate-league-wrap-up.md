# Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty Corporate League Wrap Up

  * Michael Swensen
  * Mar 23, 2022
  * 1 min read

Updated: Mar 6, 2024

  

![The winning team holding the trophy](/assets/images/blog/kamigawa-neon-dynasty-corporate-league-wrap-up-0.jpg)

Our _Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty_ Corporate League has come to an end. Thanks to all the people from all the various companies that participated and a special thanks to the Lendio team for hosting the event! We had a few companies with only one or two participants, so we joined them together one team. Well, that's the team that won! Congratulations to the team from Enso Rings, JellyFish Lighting, MX, and NICE CXone for taking the trophy this league!

  

## Join our Corporate League!

  

This league has been our largest yet. We had 628 games played over the course of the league, with seven companies with active players. It has been fun to watch this grow over time!

  

With that said, we are always looking for more people to join! The next league will be for _Streets of New Capenna_ , which is set to release on April 29th. Our league for that set begins on May 2nd and will finish with our final tournament on May 28th. Sign ups will begin in April, look for our kickoff blog for more details!
