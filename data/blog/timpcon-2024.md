# Join us at TimpCon!

  * Michael Swensen
  * Oct 10, 2024
  * 2 min read

Updated: Oct 10, 2024

  

  

![Hair on fire at computer.](/assets/images/blog/timpcon-2024-0.png)

The hot new way to network!

We’re excited to announce that **Tech Networking and Games** will be at TimpCon, hosting several awesome events! Whether you’re into Magic: The Gathering or RPGs, we’ve got something for you. You can sign up here: [_https://tabletop.events/conventions/timpcon-2024_](https://tabletop.events/conventions/timpcon-2024)!

  

  

##  _Magic: the Gathering_ Events

  

#### **Collector’s Chaos: Magic & Networking Mixer**

Join us Friday and Saturday night for a special chaos draft! For $35, you’ll receive a bag with one collector booster (from almost any set that offers them) and two additional packs from Standard sets released in the past 12 years (from Avacyn Restored onward). Draft those packs, then compete in a three-round Swiss tournament with standard prize support.

  

After the draft, keep playing! Report any games you play (tournament or casual) to earn networking points. Earn 1 point for each unique player you face, and 1 additional point for winning the game. For every 10 points you earn, you’ll receive a promo pack (while supplies last)!   

![Magic the Gathering Networking League](/assets/images/blog/timpcon-2024-1.jpg)

Corporate Networking League

#### **Commander Convergence**   

Starting Saturday at 10:00 a.m., bring your Commander decks and join the fun! We’ll set up pods, and winners of each pod will earn a promo pack. Play throughout the day and keep reporting your results. At 7:00 p.m., the top 5 players with the most points from either the Commander Convergence or Collector’s Chaos will earn an additional promo pack!
